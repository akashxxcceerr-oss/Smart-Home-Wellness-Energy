## Overview

DozAI is an end-to-end data analytics pipeline that:
1. **Forecasts** household electricity demand from real UCI smart-meter data
2. **Infers** bed/wake times passively from sub-metering patterns (no wearables)
3. **Predicts** nightly sleep quality using home-derived behavioral signals
4. **Optimizes** appliance scheduling and bedroom pre-cooling via MILP to minimize cost without harming sleep

**Pipeline:** Energy → Environment → Sleep → Prediction → Optimization → Recommendation

## Ethics & Limitations

- **Sleep labels are simulated and fully disclosed** — Nightly sleep quality scores (0–100) come from a formula calibrated to sleep-science literature, NOT real polysomnography or wearable data. Results demonstrate the optimization loop only.
- **Analytics ≠ Medical Advice** — This system identifies associations in data. It does NOT diagnose sleep disorders or recommend clinical treatment.
- **Cost savings are estimates** — Based on illustrative scenario tariff rates (Rs/kWh). Actual savings depend on your real utility contract and usage patterns.
- **Single household** — The UCI dataset represents one French household (Sceaux, 2006–2010). Generalizability is limited.
- **Separate datasets** — The energy (UCI) and sleep (Kaggle) datasets describe **different people**. The system explicitly never claims otherwise.
